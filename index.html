Crafting

Keep the fire alive

The fire is everything. If it goes out, you all lose. Feed it: select fuel, right-click the fire (Shift = whole stack).

You can only walk in light. Carry spare torches. They auto-light at any flame and only burn while you're out in the dark. Your torch lights a 3x3 around you, but only for you.

Place torches to light 5x5 for everyone. They burn out unless an Ember Root connects them to the fire.

If your light dies in the dark you have a few seconds to reach light. Otherwise you drop everything. A friend can revive you at the fire, but it costs fuel.

The fire gets hungrier over time. Weak fuel turns to ash once it's hot enough. Climb the fuel ladder: leaves, sticks, logs, peat, charcoal.

Automation

Machines touching a powered root send their output down it into the fire. Kindling Bushes and Grove Trees grow fuel in light. Rot Pits turn leaves into peat. Rain Sprouters turn rain into sticks. Kilns (from ruin blueprints) bake logs into charcoal.

Ruins out in the dark hold chests. Many have an eternal flame that never goes out.

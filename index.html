Keep the fire alive

The fire is everything. If it goes out, you all lose. Feed it: select fuel, right-click the fire (Ctrl = whole stack).

You can only walk in light. Carry torches: they light at any flame and are put away while you're in light. Out in the dark they burn one at a time and light a 3x3 around you, only for you. Run out and the dark becomes a wall.

Place torches to light 5x5 for everyone. They burn out unless an Ember Root connects them to the fire.

If your light dies in the dark you have a few seconds to reach light. Otherwise you drop everything. A friend can revive you at the fire, but it costs fuel.

The fire gets hungrier forever. Every few minutes it demands a hotter fuel, and weaker fuel turns to ash. Climb the ladder: leaves, sticks, logs, peat, charcoal, resin, lignite, heartwood, coal, ember crystal.

Grow the fire to hold more fuel and light more ground: ring it with 8 Hearthstones (Bonfire), then 16 Fire Bricks around that (Blaze), then 24 Ember Stones (Inferno).

Automation

Buildings touching a powered root send their output down it into the fire. Kindling Bushes and Grove Trees grow fuel in light. Rot Pits turn leaves into peat; plants touching one feed it directly. A Crate beside a building stores its output instead.

Eternal flames never go out. Roots touching one glow teal: they heat torches and buildings for free, and send their output into the flame's cache for you to carry home. Teal and orange roots can never meet.

Out in the dark: ruins, dead campfires, shrines (a guaranteed blueprint), collapsed mines (coal, geodes; bring a pickaxe) and ancient groves (glowmoss, heartwood).
